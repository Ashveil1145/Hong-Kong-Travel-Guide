Victoria Harbor

Victoria Harbor is a natural deep-water harbor located between Hong Kong Island and the Kowloon Peninsula. Due to its deep waters and wide harbor, as well as its location surrounded by mountains, it is ranked alongside San Francisco Bay in the United States and the Port of Rio de Janeiro in Brazil as one of the “world’s three greatest natural harbors.”

About Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of China, located at the southern tip of the mainland. It is an important maritime and air transportation hub and a free port in the Asia-Pacific region.

Victoria Harbor, shown in the photo, is Hong Kong’s natural harbor, with the Central Business District lining both sides.

Local Specialties

City

Hong Kong has limited land and a large population, a thriving business sector, and an extremely well-developed public transportation network.

Culture

Hong Kong is a major international metropolis where Eastern and Western cultures converge.

Nature

Located on the outskirts of a densely populated city, it features highly protected wilderness areas and unique geological formations.
