Discover Hong Kong

This page will introduce popular attractions in Hong Kong.

Victoria Peak

Victoria Peak is Hong Kong’s highest peak, at an elevation of 552 meters. It was once home to the rich and powerful, and the area is still known for its exclusive residential properties.

Temple Street

The stalls sell a wide variety of items, including clothing, handicrafts, and antiques. There are also quite a few stalls offering fortune-telling services, and sometimes there are traditional Cantonese opera performances nearby.

Hong Kong Disneyland

Hong Kong Disneyland is a popular theme park. Visitors can enjoy thrilling rides, watch live shows, and meet Disney characters. It is also a classic example of Hong Kong’s blend of Eastern and Western cultures.
